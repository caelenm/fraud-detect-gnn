What the map shows

  • E10 is a 2-layer GAT. In each layer, every relation has 4 attention heads, and each head gives a node's incoming neighbours weights that sum to 1. GraphSAGE (E5) gives every neighbour the same weight, 1 / degree.
  • Thicker lines carry more weight. The map shows each relation's strongest neighbours, ranked by the weight averaged over the heads, and says how much of the total weight they carry.
  • Focus is the attention's entropy over log(degree): 1 means equal weight on every neighbour, what GraphSAGE does; lower means the weight is concentrated.
  • Layer 2 weighs the neighbours' layer-1 representations, which already mix in their own neighbours.

What it is not

  • Attention weights show where the model puts weight, not why it decides. Other weightings can give the same score (Jain & Wallace, 2019).
  • The map is of a refit of E10's fold-0 model, trained on four of the five training folds, because GAT fits are not bit-identical between runs. Its held-out fold scores within 0.005 of the run's (checked below). The node's run score is the 5-seed, 5-fold average.
  • A neighbour's label is shown only for training nodes, labels the model was trained on. Test and unlabelled neighbours are grey.
  • These models use every feature, including Amazon's f19, a probable label leak.
