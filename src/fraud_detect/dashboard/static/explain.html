What these numbers are

  • SHAP values here are exact Shapley values of a stated game: the model's log-odds, with absent features filled in from a fixed background sample of training nodes (interventional TreeSHAP). They add up exactly to each node's score. That is a property of the arithmetic, not proof the explanation is faithful; the tests below check that.
  • Neighbour influence is necessity-style: how much a test node's score changes when one neighbour is removed and its neighbour means are recomputed. It is not "the important nodes", and redundant neighbours can each look unimportant.
  • Correlated features share credit unpredictably, so rankings among them are unstable; block totals are more reliable than single columns.
  • For GNN embeddings, SHAP credits embedding dimensions, not edges or nodes.

What they are not

  • They explain the model, not the cause of fraud.
  • Amazon's f19 is a probable label leak (a helpfulness-vote count, while the label is the share of helpful votes). The "without f19" model is the substantive explanation; "all 25 features" is a leakage audit.
  • YelpChi's labels come from Yelp's own spam filter, not from verified fraud.
  • Studies with fraud analysts found explanations did not reliably improve their decisions and can raise over-reliance. This page is for understanding the models in the course, not for deciding cases.
  • None of these models uses attention, so no attention weights are shown.
